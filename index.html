RULES OF THE GAME

  • Words must have at least 4 letters.
  • Words must contain the red center letter.
  • Letters can be used more than once.
  • 4-letter words earn 1 point.
  • Words with 5 or more letters score 1 point per letter.
  • Pangrams use all 7 unique letters and earn a +7 point bonus!
