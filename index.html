How to Play

  • Words must have at least 4 letters.
  • Words must include the center stone letter.
  • Letters can be used more than once.
  • Words are scored: 4-letter words = 1 pt. Longer words = 1 pt per letter.
  • Pangrams use all 7 letters at least once and award +7 bonus points!
Rink Strategy: As you earn points, your Curling Rank increases from Beginner up to Skip and Button Master!
